TIPA Awards 2008获奖名单

2008-04-28 · Wayback 存档 · 原站地址

欧州Technical Image Press Association(TIPA)于2008年4月28日公布“TIPA Awords 2008”的34个获奖产品。

TIPA是影像技术新闻协会（Technical Image Press Association）的英文缩写。该协会由来自欧洲12个国家的约31个独立公司的摄影及影视杂志组成，这些杂志与摄影、摄像或者数字产品的生产厂家没有关系。由于TIPA组织的影响力和权威性，TIPA被认为是影像业界的奥斯卡奖。每年，TIPA 的编辑成员都会评选出被认为是欧洲过去 12 个月里最出色的影像产品。

2008年的得奖产品依次如下：

最佳入门级DSLR：Sony Alpha 200

Easy to use, easy to carry and offered at a very attractive price with its 18-70mm zoom lens, the Sony Alpha 200 is a dream camera for beginners who want to start enjoying all the benefits of D-SLR photography. Its 10.2 megapixel sensor delivers top quality images up to ISO 400 and beyond, and the autofocus system is fast and accurate. The large LCD screen gives a clear, bright display when reviewing images, and both the in-camera image stabilisation and the anti-dust system are very effective in use. A superb, low-cost camera that’s easy to use, and a worthy winner of this award.

最佳玩家级DSLR: Canon EOS 450D

With its 12.2 megapixel sensor, an ISO rating between 100 and 1600, a responsive autofocus system and superb image quality, the Canon EOS 450D is an excellent camera to discover the next step in digital photography. Offered in kit form with an 18-55 mm f/3.5-5.6 lens, the camera boasts advanced features like Live View, and features an impressive LCD screen which accurately displays the model’s crisp, clean images. Both lightweight and compact, the EOS 450D is simple to use and fast, too, with a burst rate of 3.3 frames per second.

最佳准专业级DSLR: Nikon D300

With its solid design, excellent handling and robust, weather-sealed build quality, the Nikon D300 offers professional quality at a reasonable price. The 12.3 megapixel CMOS sensor delivers stunning results – even at ISO 1600 – and it can be boosted to ISO 6400 (Hi 1.0) if needed. The camera sports a fast, responsive autofocus system, the viewfinder offers 100% coverage, the large 3-inch screen gives a bright, clear display and all the high-end features like Live View, HDMI connectivity and anti-dust protection are included. On top of these exceptional features, the D300 shoots at an impressive speed of 6 frames-per-second, and will do this in bursts of 100 consecutive JPEGs or 23 RAWs.

最佳专业DSLR：Nikon D3

This professional D-SLR is a milestone for Nikon. It’s the first camera from the company with a full-frame, ‘FX’ sensor size of 23.9x36mm. As a result of this, each pixel featured on the 12.1 megapixel CMOS sensor has a larger size, and that means the low-light performance of this camera is truly incredible. The Nikon D3 can easily be used at ISO 6400, and if needed, the ISO can be cranked up to an awesome equivalent of 25,600. The rugged, magnesium-alloy chassis provides a solid foundation for the camera, and its fast burst rate of 9 frames-per-second in full-frame mode provides the necessary speed of use for the most demanding professional.

最佳中画幅数码相机：Sinar Hy6 series

The introduction of the Sinar Hy6 series gives a new competitive edge to the professional camera market. The camera is an innovative medium format system developed for high-resolution digital backs as well as film. It covers both 6x4.5cm and the classic 6x6 cm square format, providing future options for digital backs with larger CCD-sensors. The central shutter sports a shortest exposure time of 1/1000sec while the new electromagnetic mirror drive concept ensures low-vibration operation. The ergonomic, rotating control grip with an integrated LCD display ensures great handling and precise control of settings.

最佳口袋机DC: Samsung NV24HD

This innovative camera, priced at around 300 euro, features a 24mm (equivalent) wide-angle setting on its Schneider zoom lens, and offers an 86,5mm focal length at the telephoto end. An dual image stabilizer is employed to reduce camera shake, and the 10,2 megapixel sensor sports a maximum ISO sensitivity of 3200. Other innovations include a Blink Detection mode, which recognizes the appearance of the blink of an eye and takes 3 continuous shots, so people can choose the one where their eyes are not blinking. Auto Contrast Balance to allow well-exposed images in backlit conditions, a large 2.5-inch AMOLED screen with a 180 degree viewing angle, and the option to record high definition (720p) video.

最佳小型DC: Panasonic Lumix FX-500

The FX500 is the first of a new family of compact cameras with a 5x, image-stabilized zoom that covers the 25 to 125mm range – ideal for any subject from wide-angle landscapes to telephoto portraits. The images captured by the 10.1 megapixel sensor are processed by the new Venus Engine IV, and ISO sensitivity ranges from 100 to 1600, and from 1600 to 6400 in High Sensitivity mode. The 3-inch touch-screen LCD offers an easy image composition and review. For the more advanced photographer, the FX500 offers a full manual control with an innovative MASP dial solution via the touch-screen.

最佳大变焦倍率DC:Fujifilm FinePix S100FS

The S100 FS is a ‘bridge’ camera that offers a number of features found on the Fuji Finepix S5 Pro D-SLR. Its eighth generation Super CCD sensor boasts 11.1 million pixels, and full manual control is easy to achieve for photographers who like to take charge of their exposures. With an optically-stabilized 28-400mm f/2.8-5.3 lens, the S100 FS offers a huge zoom range that covers all general photography subjects, and the 2.5-inch screen can be tilted to provide the optimum viewing angle for composition. The camera’s Film Simulation modes emulate the contrast and saturation of Velvia and Provia emulsions, and there’s a RAW format for the discerning enthusiast wanting to create the look and feel of their images from scratch.

最佳消费类摄影镜头:Canon EF-S 55-250/4-5.6 IS

This telephoto zoom offers a tremendous range of focal lengths and features an optical image stabilizer, which allows around 4 stops of light to be gained. This asset compensates for the modest aperture of f/4-5.6, and in terms of use, effectively converts a slow lens into a fast one. The optical construction of 12 lenses in 10 groups ensures that sharpness and distortion is well controlled, and on a Canon D-SLR, it offers an equivalent zoom range of 88-400mm. All this and it weighs only 390 grams, so it’s light enough for photographers to carry as a regular part of their shooting kit.

最佳准专业级镜头：Olympus Zuiko Digital ED 12-60/2.8-4 SWD

This zoom lens is part of the FourThirds system, and therefore gives a film-equivalent focal length range of 24-120mm. With its fast aperture of f/2.8-4, it’s suitable for use in all lighting conditions, and the SWD technology provides enhanced autofocus speed when used with the latest Olympus D-SLRs. The lens boasts a rugged build quality, and is constructed with 15 elements in 11 groups, providing an excellent control of sharpness at the edges. With a minimum focusing distance of just 25cm and a nicely balanced weight of 435 grams, it’s a very attractive option for all FourThirds users.

最佳专业级单反镜头：Nikon AF-S NIKKOR 14-24/2.8G ED

This ultra-wide-angle zoom offers an extensive array of shooting opportunities for professionals and photo enthusiasts looking for superb optical quality. The lens construction consists of 14 elements in 11 groups with 2 ED glass elements and 3 aspherical lenses. A newly developed Nano Crystal Coating reduces ghosting and flare between the lens element and the sensor. The lens offers a constant maximum aperture of f/2.8 at all focal lengths – allowing for shooting in low light conditions – and the close focusing distance on offer is just 0.28m/0.9ft. A rounded diaphragm opening with 9 blades makes out-of-focus elements appear more natural (Bokeh effect). The SWM (Silent Wave Motor) allows quiet and fast autofocus, and permits manual override at any time with no delay.

最佳专业级DC：Sigma DP1

The Sigma DP1 is the first compact camera with a large sensor, and the one it uses is the same size as that found in the Sigma SD14. The Foveon technology employed gives very high quality images in RAW format at ISO ratings up to 200, though the sensitivity can be increased to ISO 800 if needed. Thanks to the fixed-focal-length 28mm f/4 lens, images produced by the DP1 have a quality comparable to some D-SLRs. Elegant, stylish and pleasant to use, the DP1 boasts an external viewfinder and a slick manual focus option for precise control. This luxury compact is a new departure for the digital compact, and reveals a promising line of development for the future.

年度最具创意DC：Casio EXILIM Pro EX-F1

The Casio EXILIM Pro EX-F1 opens up new fields in photography, thanks to its incredible speed. The camera takes full size, 6 megapixel photos at 60 frames-per-second, and smaller size movie clips at speeds up to 1200 frames-per-second. This allows the user to catch each decisive moment, and even motion that the human eye cannot ordinarily perceive. The unique Slow Motion View function lets users view and capture that critical moment in slow motion during still photography. This way, users can take photographs while, on the LCD screen, the momentary action that is before their eyes is displayed in slow motion. An impressive leap forward, and a worthy winner of the Best Imaging Innovation award.

年度最佳创新设计：Manfrotto ModoSteady 585

The Manfrotto ModoSteady 585 is a smart, versatile camera support tool that can be used in a variety of ways. Primarily it’s a counter-balancing stabilizer to be used while operating a camcorder. When balanced well – taking into account the weight and the centre of gravity of the camcorder – it’s an accomplished stabilizer that offers smooth, shake-free shots when panning and tracking. It also functions as a shoulder brace – just twist the assembly and you have a firm support to lock the camera into your shoulder. If that’s not enough, it can also be used as a table-top tripod – simply fold out the handle and a firm tripod for stills or video is created. The ModoSteady is truly a multi-functional camera support system that not only offers great versatility, but also works very effectively in all its guises.

最佳平板图象扫描仪:Canon CanoScan 8800F

The Canon CanoScan 8800F provides consumers with a solution for all photo archiving needs with the ability to scan up to twelve 35mm slides or negatives at a time, along with standard documents and images. The maximum optical resolution is 4800x9600 ppi. The CanoScan 8800F is equipped with Film Automatic Retouching and Enhancement (FARE) Level 3 technology, which aids in automatic dust, scratch, fading and graininess reduction. The CanoScan 8800F is the first Canon scanner to come equipped with a high-luminance white LED lamp, and this technology virtually eliminates warm-up time and allows for scanning to begin instantaneously from its sleep mode.

最佳小幅面照片打印机:HP Photosmart A826 - Home Print Centre

With its 7-inch screen and its unique ‘small TV’ appearance, the HP Photosmart 826 distinguishes itself from its competitors. The integral card reader accepts all main card formats currently used in the market, and you just have to insert a card to see the images it contains. A touch-sceen replaces the navigation buttons found on most small format printers, and all selection, print, and image manipulation commands are made by touching the screen with a finger or the included stylus. With good looks and a slick design, the Photosmart 826 delivers prints of an excellent quality for family use.

最佳专业型照片打印机: Epson Stylus Photo R1900

The Epson Stylus Photo R1900 is an A3+ printer for the demanding user that features 7 separate colours. It produces prints up to 13 inches wide and 44 inches long on a broad variety of media types including gloss, lustre, matte, canvas and fine-art papers. The 1440 nozzle print head allows a maximum resolution of 5760x1440 dpi. Epson's new UltraChrome Hi-Gloss 2 ink system gives a wider colour gamut for natural skin tones in portrait subjects, and the smooth gloss finish with the extra gloss optimizer ink renders a superb look and feel to prints. The additional red and orange inks contribute to an improved colour vibrancy, and extra features include options for CD/DVD printing and roll paper support for panoramic prints.

最佳A4幅面多功能照片打印机:Canon PIXMA MP970

Photos and more besides! The flagship model of Canon’s multifunction inkjet printer range combines high grade photo printing capabilities with advanced scanning and copying features. The multifunction device uses 7 inks in separate cartridges for natural colour rendition, and Canon’s 9600x2400 dpi FINE printhead ensures superb photo quality in terms of colour reproduction, detail and sharpness. The scanning function offers a maximum resolution of 4800 ppi, and the MP970 connects to printers via USB or an Ethernet network. Users can also take advantage of direct printing from cameras via PictBridge or from a memory card, and the 3 inch colour display and the Scroll Button offers convenient control in operation.

最佳大幅面打印机: Epson Stylus Pro 11880

The Epson Stylus Pro 11880 is a professional printer developed for large format applications including fine-art printing, photography and proofing, and it offers a maximum printing width of 64 inches. The Epson Ultrachrome K3 Vivid Magenta Ink Technology offers two new magenta inks ensuring a more precise colour reproduction – especially in difficult-to-match red and blue areas of the colour spectrum. Depending on the media used, the printer offers dedicated channels for both photo and matte black inks. Epson's Micro Piezo Thin-Film Print head ejects approximately 40,000 droplets of ink per second for high-speed printing. With a roll paper rest for easy loading, the Stylus Pro 11880 supports media up to 1.5mm thick, including glossy, matte, fine-art and plain paper, matte board, and cut-sheet stock.

最佳艺术型喷墨照片打印纸：Ilford Galerie Gold Fibre Silk

In line with the market transition from the analogue darkroom to the digital arena, Ilford has set a new standard by producing the equivalent of a classic fibre-based silver image print using inkjet technology. Ilford Galerie Gold Fibre Silk is a 310gsm inkjet paper featuring a traditional barium sulphate (baryta) base. The baryta enhances the media surface which gives the creamy whites and velvety blacks that form part of the unique look and feel of a true fibre print. The media also produces real high definition images with an extended tonal range, making it a must for exhibition-quality images ranging from vivid colour to dramatic black & white prints.

最佳图象存储:SanDisk Extreme Ducati range

Enthusiasts are taking more and more high-resolution images and videos, and the SanDisk Ducati range of memory cards features faster performance to meet their demands. The Ducati range offers CF and SD memory cards and USB 2.0 memory sticks with top-of-the-line speeds. The SanDisk Extreme Ducati Edition SD Plus card is both a high-speed 4GB SD card and a USB drive at the same time – a hinged cover exposes a USB 2.0 plug for connecting directly to a PC. The sequential read/write speeds go up to an impressive 20MB/second. The SanDisk Extreme Ducati Edition CompactFlash card is a top choice for professional photographers, and is available in 4GB and 8GB capacities, with up to 45 MB/second sequential read/write speed.

最佳图像软件：Apple Aperture 2

Today’s photographers face the task of managing and organizing an efficient digital workflow from capture right through to a variety of imaging outputs. Apple Aperture 2 is a solution that has been developed from the ground up to make this possible. Besides an improved, easier to use interface and enhanced project handling, Aperture 2 introduces a set of new imaging tools for highlight recovery, colour vibrancy, local contrast definition, soft-edged retouching, vignetting control and the fine-tuning of RAW files. The new Quick Preview mode provides fast photo browsing to allow quicker comparing and rating of images, and the open plug-in architecture makes it easy for photographers to use third-party software directly from within the programme.

最佳色彩管理系统：X-Rite ColorMunki Photo

The ColorMunki Photo gives photographers more colour control for their images. It is designed for the digital workflow of enthusiast and professional photographers and delivers an integrated solution for matching colours from display right through to final print. It combines a spectrophotometer measuring device with an intuitive software workflow. The device can calibrate displays, projectors and printers, and measures ambient light as well as capturing spot colours. The AppSet Utility included automatically activates the measured printer profile for the various user applications supported. ColorMunki Photo comes with additional colour creation and communication tools, such as ColorPalette for extracting colours from images. The DigitalPouch utility helps to ensure that viewers can look at an image in ‘safe’ colour conditions.

最佳摄影附件：Metz Mecablitz 48 AF-1 Digital

With the 48 AF-1 Digital accessory flash, Metz has shown that fully-featured flashguns can be made at a price the enthusiast can afford. The flash is compatible with all major camera brands, and boasts a guide number of 48 (ISO 100 at 105mm). The auto-zoom head is motorized to cover focal lengths from 24-105mm and the flash offers both bounce and swivel functions for creative use. Also provided is a mini-reflector and a diffuser that extends the coverage as wide as 18mm, and the flash recycling time is swift at just 0.1 - 4secs (depending on power output). The firmware for the unit can be updated through its built in USB interface, so the user can be sure that this flash will perform well not only now, but also in the future.

最佳数码附件：Wacom Cintiq 12WX

The Wacom Cintiq 12WX is an interactive pen display combining wireless pen technology with a high-resolution LCD monitor. Images can be manipulated directly on the display, allowing a more intuitive workflow when retouching or drawing. With 1280x800 pixels, 180 candela/sqm and a contrast ratio of 600:1, the Cintiq 12WX offers a stunning visual interface. The electromagnetic resonance method employed delivers a resolution of 5080 lines-per-inch and an ultra-fast response time of just 25 milliseconds. Touch-strip controllers at the left and the right side of the tablet allow the fingers to be used for additional control, extra ExpressKeys can be custom-assigned with keyboard functions and commands, and the Cintiq 12WX supports multi-display environments, too.

最佳数码相框：Sony DPF-V900/DPF-V700

The Sony S-Frames DPF-V900 (9 inch display) and DPF-V700 (7 inch display) offer WVGA resolution (800x480 pixels) along with 15:9 aspect-ratio screens. Sony’s Bionz processor with face detection technology allows the rapid display of large files, and also provides image corrections like focus enhancement, red-eye reduction and exposure adjustment. Digital images can be loaded onto the internal memory from various types of memory cards or via the USB cable from a PC or camera. The DPF-V900 and DPF-V700 digital photo frames support JPEG, TIFF, BMP and Sony RAW image file formats, and functions can be managed remotely with the included controller.

最佳高端数码投影仪：Panasonic PT-AX200E

The Panasonic PT-AX200E digital projector is ideal for viewing movies or for use as a ‘digital slide projector’. The powerful 2,000-lumen brightness and new Light Harmonizer 2 technology make it easy for people to enjoy vibrant, dynamic images, even if they don't have a special viewing room. Panasonic’s advanced technologies improve colour reproduction accuracy in movies, delivering images with a refined “Hollywood picture quality,” making this projector ideal as the heart of a home cinema system. The sophisticated colour profiling makes it possible to adapt the projector output to the users’s personal preferences, and also allow accurate and neutral photo reproduction on screen.

最佳专业数码投影仪：JVC DLA-HD100

Based on the TIPA Award-winning DLA-HD1 model, the new high-end JVC DLA-HD100 features enhanced 0.7inch, full HD D-ILA devices and wire grid optical engine. This enables the projector to further improve contrast to twice the ratio of the best conventional projectors and attain an impressive native contrast ratio of 30,000:1, without the use of an iris. The DLA-HD100 is compatible with the latest HDMI version 1.3 (Deep Colour) specifications, which allow it to go to billions of colours and reproduce subtler shades of grey. The amazing picture detail, thanks to its 1080p resolution, and the exceptional native contrast ratio ensure stunning vividness and accuracy of colour.

最佳胶卷：Kodak Professional New T-MAX 400

The latest version of Kodak’s famous black & white emulsion proves that film still has much to offer photographers. The changes are subtle rather than spectacular, but the improvements in sensitivity and sharpness are clear. This ISO 400 film thanks to its fine-grain structure can easily compete with much slower, ISO 100 films. This makes the new T-Max 400 a truly versatile all-rounder. Its ISO speed is fast enough for quick-fire reportage work in low light, yet its fine-grain emulsion yields the detail required by landscapers. An excellent film that will be enjoyed by monochrome devotees across the board.

最佳消费级DV：Panasonic HDC-SD9

With this 3 CCD, full HD camera Panasonic has succeeded in bringing a very compact model into the market. On top of the excellent image quality, this camcorder also has advanced features like face detection and the unique Intelligence Shooting guide. In spite of its diminutive size, the camera sports a total of five microphones for a truly stunning sound performance. The Panasonic HDC-SD9 has a full HD resolution of 1920x1080, and the Leica Dicomar lens and the advanced O.I.S. ensure very sharp and vibrant images. Other features are its ability to operate in minimum lighting of just 5 lux, superb picture quality at the 16:9 aspect ratio, a 10x optical zoom lens and a 2.7 inch LCD screen. This camera stores images and video on SD card. It has a weight of only 275 grams.

最佳掌上影像设备：Nokia N82

The N82 by Nokia is a compact multimedia computer that integrates phone, GPS, web, video and music functions around a 5 megapixel camera with a high-speed, f/2.8 autofocus lens. This Carl Zeiss Tessar focuses from 10cm to infinity and produces sharp prints up to and beyond A4 size. A 20X digital zoom is available to help users get closer to the subject, and though the exposure is automatic, it can be adjusted with exposure compensation of +/- 2EV. As in a traditional camera, the user can select different white balance settings to shoot accurate colours, even in fluorescent or tungsten lighting. Several scene modes are also available to optimize the N82 for great images, whether shooting portraits or night shots. This 112g mobile device adopts a Xenon flash and comes with a 100MB of internal memory, but this can be expanded to 8GB with a microSD card.

最佳数码冲印设备：KODAK Adaptive Picture Exchange

The Kodak Apex system provides retailers with unprecedented ease-of-use, flexibility and scalability, enabling them to produce profitable, high-quality photo products while minimizing cost and complexity. Apex is a totally new design in dry lab systems, designed to meet the evolving needs of retailers of any size in any channel. Retailers can connect current KODAK Order Stations to the system to fulfill consumers’ standard print orders, and can also tap the system’s ability to accept, manage and produce premium product orders like posters, single- and double-sided photobooks, calendars, collages, greeting cards, and KODAK Picture Movie DVDs.

最佳自助影像服务设备：Sony Compact Picture Station

The Sony Compact Picturestation Photo Printing Kiosk gives retailers more flexibility in managing their floor space, with a smaller footprint and an expandable kiosk system. The system can produce up to 800 prints per hour, a volume that can be easily expanded to generate more than 2,000 prints per hour. In addition, the model is the only compact footprint kiosk to feature an integrated photo scanner and the ability to print most common photo sizes (wallet, 3.5x5, 4x6, 5x7, 4x8 photo cards, 8x8, 8x10, 8x12). The system can be deployed as an expandable countertop unit, or with its base station for full functionality.

最佳显示器：Samsung SyncMaster XL30 LED BLU

Samsung’s Syncmaster XL30 is designed for colour-sensitive commercial applications like desktop publishing, photography, video editing and graphic design. This impressive display is equipped with a 30 inch Light Emitting Diode (LED) Back Light Unit (BLU) panel. Unlike conventional backlighting technology, LED improves black levels and increases the overall contrast. The display sports a high resolution of 2560x1600, combined with a contrast ratio of 1000:1, and this allows the SyncMaster XL30 to deliver crisp, clear images and motion graphics. Owing to its 14-Bit colour processing and 10-Bit output, the XL30 covers the full Adobe RGB colour space/gamut. It is enhanced by a colour management system that allows accurate colour calibration to satisfy the needs of the most demanding professionals.

最佳照片服务：CEWE Photobooks

Photobooks are becoming more and more popular with enthusiasts who want to make more of their digital images. With its photobook concept, CEWE offers a complete solution for creating and ordering high quality photobooks across Europe. Digital photos can be combined with backgrounds, text and page design, and the CEWE photobook software is free of charge and offers intuitive handling. The range of default themes on offer includes Travel and Chronicle layout designs, so users can quickly and easily create photobooks according to their needs. For advanced users, a professional version of the software with enhanced features is also available. Once the photobook has been created, the order can be uploaded online or saved to CD and handed in to any CEWE retail partner.

最佳数码伴侣：LaCie 2big Triple

The LaCie 2big Triple is a performance enhanced storage device with three interfaces for high-speed data transfer; FireWire 800, FireWire 400 or USB 2.0. The 2big Triple is a complete RAID solution, which can be individually configured for performance or safety. If requiring optimum speed and capacity, Fast mode (Raid 0) can be chosen to simultaneously write to both disks without redundancy. If data security is considered more important, Safe 100 mode (RAID 1) automatically mirrors data by splitting capacity into two identical volumes. If a drive fails while in this mode, insertion of a new drive automatically rebuilds data without any downtime.
