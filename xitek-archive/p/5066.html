Louis.Lam2003-10-22 10:355 分
操作性 5.00耐用性 5.00性价比 5.00解像力 5.00色彩还原 5.00机械结构 5.00
总评

One of the best 90mm Elmarit that Leica offers. It is espically suitable for travel and still very useful many other application. Tack sharp and contrasty at f/2.8 till f/11, at all distance. Clean and rich color due to 4-element design. The latest version has muilti-coatings (green-ish + deep yellow) which enchance the color and reduce the flare. Focus shift problem is not much by attached filter and aperture settings. However, don't know how much focus shift when using infra-red film.

优点

-Sharpness and clean color contrast. -Very reasonable priced after the APO-90 has released. -Very sharp even enlarged till 30X. -Leica/Leitz spends much time to improves the 4-element 90mm design. It is the ultimate result from more than 40 yrs of R&D. The latest Marco-Elmar is also a 4-element design, which is said to be outperform the 100 APO-Elmarit-R in terms of resolution.

缺点

-Silver chrome version really heavy, but very handsome look. -Black anodise version is very handy, but not so good looking. Very difficult to choose, Ha Ha ... :D -Have different coatings version. All Chrome version is muilt-coated. Early Black anodise have mono-coatings, very late one is also muilti-coated. All are great performers.
